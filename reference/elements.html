Text

Body text is 16px with a line height of 1.5. Most reading happens here, so it gets the most room. A paragraph can hold a link, strong text, emphasis, inline code, a keyboard shortcut like ⌘ K, highlighted text, an abbr, and small print.

A second paragraph follows with the standard flow spacing. Paragraphs, lists, tables, and figures all share the same bottom margin, and the last child in a container drops it.

Icons

Phosphor, regular weight, pasted as inline SVG with aria-hidden="true". They take the size and color of the text around them.

Inline in a sentence the icon sits on the baseline. In a button, put the label on the button and hide the icon.
